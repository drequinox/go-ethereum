# Review context

Copy this file to `context.md` in your workspace root (applies to every review) or in a review folder (applies to that review only; it adds to or overrides the workspace one). Delete lines that do not apply. The review uses this to rate severity and choose depth; it does not limit which risks are considered.

- **System:** [what it is and what it does, for example: institutional custody platform covering tenants, organisations, wallets and withdrawals]
- **Users:** [who uses it, for example: institutional clients' admins and approvers; internal staff operators]
- **Value at risk:** [assets or amounts at stake, at launch and expected]
- **Chains and tokens:** [every chain and token standard supported or planned, for example: Bitcoin, Ethereum and Arbitrum, Solana (SPL and Token-2022), XRPL; staking on which chains]
- **Key management:** [MPC vendor, HSM, multisig; hot, warm and cold split]
- **Hosting and vendors:** [cloud and regions; custody, KYC, KYT and other vendors]
- **Regulation and policy:** [for example MiCA, DORA, internal custody policy, CCSS level targeted]
- **Out of scope:** [flows already reviewed (with review IDs), components owned elsewhere]
- **Known decisions and accepted risks:** [anything already agreed that the review should not reopen]
- **Go-live:** [date, and anything that must be true by then]

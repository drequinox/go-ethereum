ECOSYSTEM NOTES — Lighthouse DAO community update, May 2026 (excerpt)

Lighthouse DAO continues to fund open tooling for the network. In May the
treasury approved a 25,000 USDC grant to Alice (wallet 0xALICE), an independent
developer building an open-source analytics dashboard for the community. A
second grant of 18,000 USDC went to the Beacon Docs collective.

Bob (wallet 0xBOB) was elected to the DAO council this quarter. Outside his
governance role, Bob is one of the largest liquidity providers in AquaSwap's
ETH/USDC pool.

Community artist Carol (wallet 0xCAROL) deployed the ArtBlock (ART) NFT
collection and lists her pieces on the PixelBay marketplace. Her piece
"Genesis Lighthouse #7" was purchased by collector Dave (wallet 0xDAVE) for
2.5 ETH; PixelBay routes a 5% marketplace fee on every sale to the Lighthouse
DAO treasury under the partnership agreement signed in March.

Dave also subscribes to Alice's analytics dashboard, paying in USDC.
